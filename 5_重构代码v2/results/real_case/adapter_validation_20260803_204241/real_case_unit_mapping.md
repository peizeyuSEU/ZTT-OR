# Real-case unit mapping

- Demand: tonnes/year or tonnes/day as explicitly named.
- Distance: km.
- Transport rate: CNY/(tonne-km); transport baseline fields are CNY/tonne and equal rate times distance.
- Transport emission factor: tCO2e/(tonne-km).
- Facility emissions: tCO2e/year; inventory emissions: tCO2e/(tonne-year).
- Prices and costs: CNY/tonne, CNY/year, CNY/order as named in source headers.
- No conversion or duplicate distance multiplication is performed by the adapter.
